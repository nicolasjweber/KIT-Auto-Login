# Privacy Policy

## Data Collection
KIT Auto-Login does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data, login credentials, or browsing history, neither locally nor to any external server.

## Login Credentials
This extension does **not** handle, read, or store your passwords or passkeys. It relies entirely on your password manager or your manual input to fill in credentials. It only automates the navigation steps, like clicking the correct login buttons.
