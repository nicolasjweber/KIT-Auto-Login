# Privacy Policy for KIT Auto-Login

## Data Collection
The "KIT Auto-Login" browser extension does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data, login credentials, or browsing history to any external server.

## Login Credentials
This extension does **not** handle, read, or store your passwords. It relies entirely on your browser's built-in password manager or your manual input to fill in credentials. It only automates the navigation steps (clicking "Login" buttons).

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not use any analytics, tracking, or third-party services.
